What the model uses

The estimate is produced by a Random Forest regressor with nine inputs:

  • Neighborhood / location
  • Living area (sq ft)
  • Lot size (sq ft)
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Property age in years
  • Floors / stories
  • Parking / garage capacity
  • Property type

Reading your result

The result page shows the estimated price, the implied price per square foot, and a sensitivity table showing how the estimate moves when one characteristic is changed and the rest are held constant.

Sensitivity is a what-if view of the model's response, not a causal effect and not a guarantee.

Scope of the data

Training records cover residential sales in {{ app_globals.dataset.market_region }} recorded between {{ app_globals.dataset.observation_period }}. Prices are in US dollars and reflect that market and period only.
